I am making a daft punk helmet, the silver robots helmet to be exact but i want to put my own spin on it. My plan to differentiate it is to use a matte black color/texture and have the LEDs be orange (most of the time). I found a file online of the helmets model and i am using that so my life doesnt suck and i have to make an accurate model myself. I printed out the model after scaling it up in one direction and it seems... a bit big to say the least. I think that this actually may be fine because i have to shove all the electronics and the battery bank in there, so once those and the foam etc go in the size may actually be a boon.
