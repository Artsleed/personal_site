I created a website to allow Queen's students to test out and design their jackets.

I remember wanting to get a bunch of bars sewn on but being unsure of the order, placement and selection i wanted to do.

I thought that it would be nice to have some sort of tool that would let me try out different combinations and designs.

So, after i finally got the motivation to, i built that tool and while building thought that others may have had the same issue that i did. Once i thought to share, the site exploded with new feature to try and accommodate as many people as i could.

As of writing this the site includes nearly every bar there is (non custom ones that is), color and wear settings, the ability to create custom bars, flags, and crests. The site has both 2D and 3D views available and saves in the browser in case the site is accidently closed

Going forward i want the site to evolve and when i get the time and see fit i will be adding new features and options.

This project was tons of fun to work on and it was really satisfying to make something that i can see myself and others really using somewhat regularly.
